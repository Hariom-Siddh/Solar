Clear, transparent answers about solar installation, residential subsidies, commercial tax benefits, and maintenance in Sikar.

Under the central government scheme, residential rooftop installations receive ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW, and up to ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above. Our team in Sikar registers your application on the national portal and manages DISCOM inspections so the subsidy amount is credited directly to your bank account within 30 days of meter activation.
Commercial and industrial customers in Sikar enjoy 40% Accelerated Depreciation under Section 32 of the Income Tax Act in the very first year! You can also claim full GST input tax credit, reducing the effective project cost drastically while lowering expensive commercial electricity tariff units down to near zero.
Standard On-Grid systems safely disconnect during grid outages for lineman safety. If your area near Railway Station Road or Kalyan Circle experiences regular power cuts, we install a Hybrid Solar System or connect your rooftop panels to an Inverter & Battery pack, ensuring 100% continuous backup with zero flicker for fans, lights, and appliances.
